PEAK (Modded) for Mac

This app contains only our own code (MIT licence, LICENSE-PEAK-Modded.txt).

When you set it up, it downloads, separately and from pinned, hash-checked sources:
- the PeakPack Wine runtime (Wine from CodeWeavers' CrossOver LGPL sources, DXMT, DXVK, MoltenVK,
  and the Steam bridge). Each runtime carries its own LICENSES folder, NOTICE.md and SOURCES.md with
  the LGPL source offer: github.com/jmproulx/peakpack, releases "mac-build-runtime-*";
- PeakPack.exe and the mods, from Joshua's signed mod pack (github.com/jmproulx/peakpack) and Thunderstore.

It never downloads or contains PEAK's game files: Steam downloads them with your own account.
Steam, PEAK, Unity and macOS are their owners' products; this app is not affiliated with them.
